# Mock Asset Generation Direction

These assets were generated as original temporary development media. The production files contain no added words, logos, watermarks, UI, or copied official artwork.

## Shared rules used for every prompt

- Editorial rather than ecommerce or generic marketing.
- Sharp rectangular crop; no rounded frame.
- No people or recognizable actors.
- No readable text, title treatment, logo, label, or watermark inside the image.
- Original visual only; no imitation of official film posters, album covers, museum images, or reference-site assets.
- DROP palette: near-black, off-white, restrained orange/purple, plus material-specific color.
